INFINITY OS

Temporary private development host. Owner Linux and Android controls work. Linux Files and Browser open the retained private desktop. Notes, Calendar and Music open Android apps. Music was set to device-files-only. Android now has an owner-approved Google account, app-specific login is separate. Android audio is not forwarded to this browser. Cloning and Android restore are not implemented. Not production hosting. Mobile WebKit touch is tested. Actual iPhone behavior is not yet confirmed. For iPhone edge-to-edge launch: Safari Share > Add to Home Screen, enable Open as Web App, then open that icon. Browser fullscreen support varies; the ⤢ button requests native fullscreen where allowed. Aspect ratio is preserved without cropping. This Android image has Google Play services and a linked Google account, but its com.android.vending is LicenseChecker, not a usable Play Store.
